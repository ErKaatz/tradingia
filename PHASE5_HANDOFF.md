## What could justify reopening

Only genuinely new independent evidence: a separately supported economic mechanism, a new enabling dataset, a material measurable market-structure change, or strong new empirical evidence. Reopening requires one family, at most two hypotheses, provenance, temporal policy, fresh applicable cost calibration, preregistration, tests, and explicit authorization before performance.

Never infer that FX has no edge, that a viable cell has an edge, that invalid runs are evidence, or that a rejected result authorizes a rescue. Do not auto-resume research.
