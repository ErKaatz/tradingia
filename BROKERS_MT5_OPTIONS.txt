BROKERS — MT5 OPTIONS (documentation only, not a decision)
============================================================

This file is a shortlist for future reference. It is NOT a recommendation,
NOT a regulatory claim, and NOT wired into any code. No broker is frozen
or selected. Nothing here should be treated as verified/current — brokers
change conditions, entities, and regulatory status over time.

Two rough categories under consideration:

1) Cent accounts — candidates for an eventual micro-live experiment
   (see LIVE_TRADING_RULES.md: max USD 10 initial funding, demo-proven
   infrastructure only, no live trading during Phase 0):
     - HFM Cent
     - Exness Standard Cent
     - RoboForex ProCent

2) Raw/ECN accounts — candidates for a later stage, if a strategy ever
   earns enough evidence to justify tighter spreads / different cost
   structure than a Cent account offers:
     - Pepperstone Razor
     - FP Markets Raw
     - Tickmill Raw
     - IC Markets

Nothing above is final. Before depositing any real money with any broker,
verify directly with the broker (not from memory, not from this file):

  - Exact legal entity that would hold the account, and whether it
    accepts clients resident in Paraguay.
  - Regulator / license under which that specific entity operates.
  - Minimum deposit.
  - Contract size and minimum lot per symbol of interest.
  - Real commission (not just the advertised headline rate).
  - Realistic observed spread (not just the advertised minimum).
  - Whether Expert Advisors / algorithmic trading via the MT5 Python API
    are permitted under that account's terms.
  - Withdrawal process and any onboarding/KYC requirements.

No claim about any of the above (legality, licensing, tax treatment,
suitability) is made or implied by this file. This is a starting point
for manual due diligence, not a substitute for it.
