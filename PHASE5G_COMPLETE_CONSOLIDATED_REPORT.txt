2. TRACK A — OBSERVABILITY CONTRACT
==========================================================================

Source document:
PHASE5G_OBSERVABILITY_CONTRACT.md

Implementation:
src/fx/research/contracts.py

Future research persists a deterministic bundle:

  manifest.json
  summary.json
  trades.jsonl

ResearchTradeRecord is strategy-neutral and includes:

  experiment/run/variant/split identity
  symbol, timeframe, side and lots
  signal, entry and exit UTC timestamps
  reference, bid, ask and execution prices at entry and exit
  gross PnL, spread, slippage, commission, swap and net PnL
  bars held, duration and terminal-insolvency context
  derived entry/exit UTC hours

The economic identity is enforced fail-closed:

  net_pnl = gross_reference_pnl - spread_cost - slippage_cost
            - commission_cost - swap_cost

ExperimentManifest requires source-control identity, dataset/cost/non-spread/
preregistration fingerprints, symbol/timeframe/split, exact variant registry,
execution/account assumptions and research-rules reference. Serialization is
strict canonical JSON: no NaN, Infinity or -Infinity. This enables future
attribution by side, month, hour, duration, costs and sequence without reruns.

Phase 5D/5E artifacts were not altered or rerun.

4. FROZEN MICROSTRUCTURE DEFINITIONS AND LABELS
==========================================================================

Source implementation:
src/fx/research/phase5g_capture.py
src/fx/research/phase5g_viability.py

The report calculates only descriptive data-quality and microstructure fields:

  availability, bar count, validation/gaps and coverage;
  spread median, P75, P95 and P99;
  absolute close-to-close movement and high-low bar range;
  median and P95 spread-to-range burden;
  UTC-hour microstructure when at least 50 observations exist.

Normalization is dimensional:

  spread_price = spread_points * broker symbol point
  burden = spread_price / bar_range_price

No cross-symbol conversion is guessed where metadata would be ambiguous.

Frozen quality/label rule:

  adequate data = >= 500 bars, coverage >= 0.98, no errors,
                  and <= 50 unexpected gaps

  VIABLE_FOR_FUTURE_RESEARCH:
    adequate and median spread/range <= 0.25
    and P95 spread/median-range <= 1.00

  BORDERLINE:
    adequate and median spread/range <= 0.50
    and P95 spread/median-range <= 2.00

  otherwise STRUCTURALLY_UNATTRACTIVE
  failed availability rule = INSUFFICIENT_DATA

These labels measure microstructure burden only. They do not mean profitable,
unprofitable, strategy candidate, trade instruction or best market.
